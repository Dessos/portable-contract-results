## Limits that remain material

The synthetic authority bindings were stipulations. Real institutional recognition, source completeness in a working business process, practical benefit and production reliability are unestablished.

The native and adapted directions differ. Retaining complete sender programs does not mean the receiver executed them or supports their general language. The tests used finite, fixed supported meanings and explicit equivalent modeled inputs.

Runtime isolation and chronology relied partly on controller attestations. The independent summaries preserve their lack of independent live OS/file-open witnessing and other source-custody limits. An earlier Beta staging error and separately authorized recovery remain part of the wider history; no history is silently regraded here.

A12's correct rejection of insufficient lineage does not recover its missing dependency. Successful negative tests and successful reconstruction of valid covered artifacts are different claims.

No current demonstrated disagreement justifies profile revision. A public repository creates an inspectable research object; it is not another conformance result.

## Reproduction boundary

The frozen ZIP contains the two Markdown specifications and their manifest. It contains no complete executable fixture suite or implementations.

The selected JSON summaries are reports of past bounded work. Reproducing those studies would require a separately prepared and reviewed evidence release. A fresh independent implementation would need identical approved inputs, genuine isolation and a fixed comparison protocol; opening an issue does not commission that experiment.
