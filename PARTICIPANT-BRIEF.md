# Portable Contract Results - Participant brief

**One contract. One useful question.**

Proposed documentation-only feasibility study | 30 October 2026

We are seeking one contract owner or authorized reviewer to assess whether a useful contractual consequence can be modeled from a small set of real records, with its meaning confirmed by the appropriate people.

## The proposed question

At an agreed evaluation point, what remains of one period's minimum-purchase commitment after qualifying deliveries and attributable credits? The participant selects the actual contract and confirms the question and scope.

## Who we need

A contract owner, procurement or commercial operations lead, or legal reviewer who can identify the governing documents and establish who is recognized to confirm contractual meaning, relevant events, and record completeness. These responsibilities may belong to different people.

## A small starting source pack

The relevant clause and incorporated definitions; applicable amendments or confirmation of none; one accepted-delivery record and its linked credit/return; and the basis for recognizing the relevant reviewers and source authorities. Provide only material you are permitted to share, preserving context needed to interpret it.

This sample tests modelability. A full period balance needs the relevant complete evidence set and an accountable completeness basis.

## What participation involves

Select one useful question and describe the current reconciliation process. Review a draft separating source facts, proposed interpretations, confirmed meaning, and unresolved questions. Confirm or correct the governing state, time scope, eligibility, credit treatment and authority basis; identify who must resolve remaining judgments.

## What you receive

A short source-to-model record showing material terms, evidence, qualification links, coverage limits, and dependencies another authorized provider would need. A GO / NO-GO / NEEDS-AUTHORITY recommendation assesses feasibility, open questions, and value compared with the existing process.

## The decision gate

GO requires a useful question, confirmed material meaning and authority, and a complete accountable dependency chain for the proposed scope. Missing facts remain explicit; material disputes stay with the recognized decision-maker. Results identify any dependence on a human determination. GO recommends a separately authorized next step.

## First response

Identify a candidate workflow and the person or role able to sponsor its review before providing records. No contract, participant, or reviewer has yet been selected.

Start with a [workflow proposal issue](https://github.com/Dessos/portable-contract-results/issues/new?template=feedback.md) describing the question, source types, and reviewer role. Source records can stay with their owner until the review scope and access are agreed.

---

Scope: document review and modeling only. Participation does not authorize system integration, operational decisions, or publication of records.
